About

Writers who care how your CV reads.

Resume Cubicle helps people present their experience clearly, honestly and with confidence.

What we do

Most people undersell themselves on paper. They list duties instead of results, use vague wording, or bury their best work on page two. We fix that.

Resume Cubicle writes CVs, cover letters and LinkedIn profiles, and supports you through the job search itself. Every piece is built from your own experience and aimed at the roles you actually want.

Our aim is simple: when a recruiter spends a minute on your application, that minute should show them why you’re worth a conversation.

How we work.

Clarity

Short, direct sentences that make a point and move on. If a line doesn’t help your case, it doesn’t stay.

Honesty

We never invent jobs, qualifications or results. Strong writing comes from drawing out what’s real, not from exaggeration.

Your voice

Your documents should sound like a confident version of you. You review everything and have the final say.

Respect for your information

Your details are used to prepare your work. We never need ID numbers or financial information, so please don’t send them.

Who we write for

People at very different stages come to us, and each needs something different from their CV.

  • Graduates and first-time job seekers who have limited experience and need to make the most of projects, volunteering and skills
  • Career changers who need their past experience framed for a new field
  • Professionals aiming for a step up who need senior-level results to show clearly
  • People returning after a break who want to present their story with confidence
  • Anyone whose CV has stopped working and wants a fresh pair of eyes

What we don’t do

We don’t promise you a job, because no writer honestly can. What we can do is give you strong, accurate documents and a clear plan, which improves your chances.

We also don’t use one template for everyone. A CV for a nurse, a software developer and a sales manager should not look or read the same.
