Process

A clear process, with you in it.

No black box. You know what happens next, what we need from you, and where you can step in.

Four steps from first message to final draft.

You stay in the loop at every step.

  1. 1

    Share your goals

    Send your current CV if you have one, the roles you want and the achievements you’re proudest of.

    Use the form on the contact page. Rough notes are fine, so don’t worry about polishing anything. The more specific you are about the roles you’re aiming for, the sharper the writing will be.

    • Your current CV, if you have one
    • Job titles or listings you’re aiming for
    • Achievements you’re proud of
  2. 2

    Get your first draft

    Your documents are written around your strengths and the jobs you’re aiming for.

    Team Jareh may ask follow-up questions along the way to draw out detail: numbers, scale, timeframes, who benefited. These details are what turn a list of duties into evidence of results.

  3. 3

    Refine together

    Review, ask for changes and polish until every line sounds like you.

    Read the draft as if you were the recruiter. Flag anything that feels untrue, off-tone or missing. Your feedback shapes the final version, and nothing goes out that you aren’t comfortable with.

  4. 4

    Apply with confidence

    Take your CV, cover letter and LinkedIn profile into your search, ready to be noticed.

    If job search support is part of your plan, you’ll also get guidance on where to apply, how to follow up and how to keep track of it all.

Before you get in touch.

You don’t need to arrive with everything ready. Here is what helps, and what doesn’t matter yet.

Helpful to have

  • Your current CV or a rough list of your jobs and dates
  • Links or text of roles you’d like to apply for
  • A few results you’re proud of, big or small
  • Any deadlines you’re working to

Not needed yet

  • A finished or well-formatted CV
  • Exact numbers for every achievement
  • Total certainty about which job you want
  • Any ID numbers, bank details or other sensitive information

What to expect.

Clear communication

Team Jareh replies to the email you give. You’ll know when to expect your next update and what is needed from you.

Honest writing

Your documents only say what’s true. If something is an estimate, we check it with you before it goes in.

Realistic timelines

Timing depends on the services you choose and how quickly you can review drafts. It’s confirmed before work begins.
