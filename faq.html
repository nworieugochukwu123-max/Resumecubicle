FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Send a message and Team Jareh will reply directly.

Getting started

What do I need to send?

Your current CV if you have one, the job titles or listings you’re targeting, and a few achievements you’re proud of. No CV yet? That’s fine, just tell Team Jareh about your experience.

I don’t have a CV. Can you still help?

Yes. Many people start from nothing. Share your work history, education, skills and anything you’re proud of, even as rough notes, and Team Jareh will shape it into a complete CV.

Can my CV be tailored to a specific job?

Yes. Share the job description and your documents can be written to match what the employer is looking for.

How do I get started?

Use the form on the contact page with your name, email and a few details. Your message goes straight to Team Jareh, who will reply to the email you give.

Your documents

Will my LinkedIn match my new CV?

That’s the idea. Your CV, cover letter and LinkedIn profile tell one consistent story, so nothing contradicts anything else.

Will my CV work with applicant tracking systems?

We use a clean, simple layout and include the keywords from your target roles, which is what these systems rely on. Nobody can promise how a specific employer’s system will treat a document, but clear structure and relevant wording give you the best chance.

Will the writing sound like me?

It should. We ask questions and work from your own words and experience, and you review every draft. If a line doesn’t sound like you, say so and it will be changed.

Do you make things up to make my CV look stronger?

No. Everything comes from your real experience. We ask questions to draw out detail you may have forgotten, but we don’t invent jobs, qualifications or results. If a figure is an estimate, we’ll check it with you.

Working together

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Review and refinement is part of the process. You read the draft, tell us what to adjust, and we polish it until you’re happy.

How long does it take?

It depends on which services you choose and how quickly you can review drafts. Team Jareh will give you an expected timeline before work begins.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on what you need. Tell us which services you’re interested in and Team Jareh will confirm the cost before you commit.

Is my information kept private?

Your details and documents are used only to prepare your work. You never need to send ID numbers, bank details or other sensitive information, so please don’t.

Your job search

Can you guarantee I’ll get a job?

No. Nobody honestly can, because hiring depends on many things outside a CV. What we can do is give you strong, accurate documents and clear guidance, which improves your chances of being noticed.

Do you write for every industry?

We write for a wide range of fields. If your industry has specific conventions, such as academic CVs or creative portfolios, mention it in your message so Team Jareh can confirm the fit.

Send a message and you’ll get a direct reply.
