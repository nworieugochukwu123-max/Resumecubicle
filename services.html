Services

Writing that does the work for you.

Choose a single service or combine them. Each one is written around your experience and the roles you want next.

01

Resume and CV writing

Your CV has one job: to get you an interview. We write it around your strongest results, in a layout that reads well to people and to applicant tracking systems.

Best for

People starting from scratch, people whose CV has grown into a long list of duties, and anyone changing direction who needs their experience framed for a new field.

What’s included

  • A conversation about the roles you’re targeting
  • A full rewrite of your experience, led by results
  • A short profile summary that states your value
  • A clean layout that’s easy to scan
  • Skills and keywords matched to your target roles
  • Review and refinement until you’re happy with it

What we’ll need from you

Your current CV if you have one, job titles or listings you’re aiming for, and the achievements you’re proudest of.

02

Cover letters

A good cover letter doesn’t repeat your CV. It explains why you, why this role and why now, in a few focused paragraphs that a busy reader will finish.

Best for

Applications where the employer asks for a letter, career changes that need a little explaining, and roles where you want to stand out from a crowded pile.

What’s included

  • A letter written for a specific role
  • Your experience linked to what the employer asks for
  • An opening that gets to the point
  • A closing that makes the next step clear
  • A tone that matches your voice and your industry

What we’ll need from you

The job description, your CV and a few lines on why the role interests you.

03

LinkedIn optimisation

Recruiters often search LinkedIn by keyword before they ever see a CV. We rework your profile so you show up in the right searches and make a strong impression once someone clicks.

Best for

People who are open to opportunities, moving into a new industry, or who haven’t touched their profile in a long time.

What’s included

  • A headline that says what you do and what you offer
  • An About section written in your voice
  • Experience entries that match your CV
  • Skills and keywords for the roles you want to be found for
  • Advice on small details that affect how visible and credible your profile looks

What we’ll need from you

A link to your current profile, your CV and the roles you want to be found for.

04

Job search support

Writing is only part of a job search. We help you decide where to look, how to apply and when to follow up, so your effort goes into the right roles.

Best for

People who are applying often but hearing little back, or who aren’t sure where to start.

What’s included

  • Help defining your target roles and employers
  • Guidance on reading job descriptions and tailoring each application
  • Advice on following up and on writing networking messages
  • A simple way to keep track of your applications

What we’ll need from you

A short description of where you are in your search and what hasn’t been working.

05

The full package

If you want everything to work together, the full package covers your CV, cover letter, LinkedIn profile and job search guidance, written as one consistent story.

Best for

Anyone beginning a serious job search who wants to apply with confidence and not rework their documents halfway through.

What’s included

  • Everything listed above
  • Matching dates, titles and wording across every document
  • One conversation to cover all of it, so you don’t repeat yourself

Pricing and timelines depend on what you need. Resume Cubicle will confirm both before any work begins.
